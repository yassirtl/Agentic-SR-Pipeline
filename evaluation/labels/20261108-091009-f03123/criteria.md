# Screening criteria: Does exercise reduce depressive symptoms in older adults?

Label each record from its title and abstract only, as a first-stage screener: `include` unless the record clearly fails a criterion (when in doubt, include). Criteria marked *full text only* cannot exclude here.

- **D**: A primary study reporting its own results, with a design that is one of: randomized controlled trial (reviews, meta-analyses incl. network meta-analyses, study protocols without results, editorials and letters do not meet D)
- **I1**: Randomized controlled trial
- **I2**: Healthy older adults aged over 60 years
- **I3**: No significant baseline difference between experimental and control groups *(full text only)*
- **I4**: Intervention is physical exercise; the control group does no regular exercise
- **I5**: Depression measured with GDS, GDS-15, SCL-90, SGDS-K, TDQ or TGDS
- **I6**: Data reported as mean and standard deviation *(full text only)*
- **E1**: Older adults with comorbidities or below 60 years of age
- **E2**: Interventions beyond exercise
- **E3**: Incomplete data or no control group
